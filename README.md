# toner-skill
This skill will look for aggressive language, presumptive language, negative reinforcement framing, and condescension in text or documents, count and flag the instances, and then remediate them with an explanation of what was changed and why. You can use toner with copy that has been pasted into your Claude CLI or environment of choice (Visual Studio etc), or by giving it a link directly to a doc. It's also possible to integrate the skill into other creation, review and editing based skills as a toggle, so that you can use it at the time of generation instead of waiting until your text has been generated.

**Usage**
**/toner** <text>
**/toner** <link to doc>
